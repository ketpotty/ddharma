# DDharma Protocol: The Distributed Anti-Fragile Eco-Commons

## Overview
DDharma (Distributed Dharma) Protocol is an open-source, decentralized, autonomous framework designed to translate the unalterable laws of ecological balance into a digital, self-enforcing network. It solves the **Tragedy of the Commons** in the context of the global climate and drought crisis by utilizing blockchain technology, satellite remote sensing, and game-theoretic incentives.

The protocol completely bypasses nation-state bureaucracies and corporate "greenwashing" mechanisms. It operates strictly as a distributed digital mirror between physical reality and global capital.

## Core Architecture: DDharma & Karma

The protocol operates on an automated feedback loop driven by two distinct systems:

1. **The DDharma Engine (Distributed Law):** A deterministic smart contract that tracks a tamper-proof **Ecological Stress Map** generated entirely by satellite data (e.g., Copernicus/NASA soil moisture indices) and cryptographic IoT hardware sensors. 
2. **The Karma Engine (Cause-and-Effect):** The behavioral subsystem that enforces consequences based on the unalterable rules of actions.
   - **Proof of Impact:** Capital deployed into the system yields non-transferable, immutable **Soulbound Tokens (Gift Credits)** representing permanent moral prestige.
   - **Zero Governance for Capital:** Capital carries 0% voting power. Governance and multi-tier verification rights are strictly bound to non-commercial Karma scores earned exclusively through physical field verification.
   - **Anti-Hoarding (Demurrage):** Liquid capital stored inside the network automatically degrades over time (-15% monthly). The only way to preserve value is to deploy it immediately into the physical drought mitigation vectors designated by the automated satellite map.
